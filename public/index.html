How to Play

You have 7 guesses to find the target country.

After each guess you'll be given data comparing the country you guessed to the target country.
A positive number indicates that the target country has a higher value.

CO2 emissions, in million tons
Average Temperature, in celsius
Land Area, in square miles
Is your guess on the right continent?
Direction from your guess to the answer

Note: In this game, the Americas are divided into two continents. South America is one continent, North and Central America is another.
